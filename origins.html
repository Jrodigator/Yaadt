THE ORIGINS OF YAADT

Long ago, beyond the borders of civilisation, there existed a mysterious land known simply as the Yaadt.

Nobody knew exactly where the Yaadt was.

Some said it was a place. Others said it was a state of mind. The oldest scholars insisted that the Yaadt was not something you could find - the Yaadt finds you.

Every year, thousands of brave Yaadtronauts would gather for the annual Yaadt Expedition, travelling across the Yaadtiverse in search of the legendary Golden Yaadt.

Their journey began at Yaadt Station, where the Yaadt Shuttle waited patiently beside the intergalactic highway. Unfortunately, the shuttle was 47 minutes late because of a minor Yaadtastrophe involving a traffic cone, three unidentified aliens, and the Yaadt Department of Transport.

Eventually, they launched.

3… 2… 1… YAADT.

The Yaadtronauts blasted through the atmosphere, past the Yaadtellite belt, through the Yaadt Nebula, and directly into Yaadt Space.

Their captain, Darth Yaadter, stood proudly at the controls.

"May the Yaadt be with us," he declared.

Unfortunately, nobody had told him that the ship was in reverse.

They crash-landed on Planet Yaadt, where they were immediately surrounded by a mysterious alien species known as the Yaadtrestrials.

"Who are you?" demanded the alien leader.

"We are the Yaadts."

The alien looked confused.

"We are also the Yaadts."

Everyone stared at each other.

It was Yaadt-ception.

After several hours of diplomatic negotiations, the two Yaadt civilisations reached an agreement known as the Yaadt Accords, establishing the United Yaadts and creating the first ever Yaadtenomics system.

The economy collapsed within twelve minutes.

Nobody knew why.

The Yaadtrestrials blamed inflation.

The Yaadtronauts blamed capitalism.

The economists blamed the Yaadt.

The Yaadt blamed nobody.

Because the Yaadt does not blame.

The Yaadt simply exists.

Eventually, the expedition reached the legendary Golden Yaadt.

It was enormous.

It was beautiful.

It was glowing with ancient cosmic energy.

The expedition leader approached it slowly.

"After all these years…"

He reached out.

He touched the Golden Yaadt.

And suddenly -

YAADT.

The universe exploded into Yaadt.

Stars became Yaadts.

Planets became Yaadts.

Galaxies became Yaadts.

Time became Yaadt.

Space became Yaadt.

Even the laws of physics were replaced by the Yaadt Laws of Physics, the first of which states:

If something can be Yaadt, it will eventually become Yaadt.

And so the Yaadt continues to expand across the Yaadtiverse.

Nobody knows when it will end.

Nobody knows where it came from.

Nobody knows what the Yaadt truly is.

But one thing is certain:

The Yaadt is inevitable.
