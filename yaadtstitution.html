THE YAADTSTITUTION

ARTICLE I: DEFINITION OF YAADT

1.1 A Yaadt is defined as anyone or anything that successfully identifies as a Yaadt.
1.2 In the event of a dispute, the object in question shall be stared at until it conforms.

ARTICLE II: RIGHTS OF YAADTS

2.1 Every Yaadt has the fundamental right to exist.
2.2 Every Yaadt has the right to refuse to elaborate.
2.3 The right to panic during a Yaadtastrophe shall not be infringed.

ARTICLE III: RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 All Yaadts must participate in the Yaadt Census, assuming the census forms have not been misplaced again.
3.2 Any Yaadt found interfering with traffic cones will be subject to an immediate stern look from the Ministry.

ARTICLE IV: CONTRADICTORY CLAUSES

4.1 This clause intentionally contradicts Clause 4.2.
4.2 This clause completely agrees with Clause 4.1, rendering both clauses legally void.

Adopted during the First Yaadt Age.
Signed by nobody, as nobody brought a pen.
