YAADTORICAL TIMELINE

ERA: UNKNOWN

THE PRE-YAADT ERA

Little is known about the period before Yaadt. Most historians agree that things were generally less confusing, but significantly more boring, like a braai without any brandy and coke.

DATE: THE BEGINNING

THE ORIGIN

The events described in Yaadchive Document 001. The Yaadt Expedition discovers the Golden Yaadt, causing a fundamental rewrite of the universe's physics.

DATE: SHORTLY AFTER

THE FIRST YAADT AGE

Something historically important happened here. Exactly what happened remains disputed by everyone involved, mostly because it happened during Stage 6 loadshedding.

DATE: DISPUTED

THE GREAT YAADT SCHISM

A profound disagreement divided Yaadt society. The cause of the disagreement is officially recorded in the Yaadchives as: "Complicated, someone brought Castle Lager instead of Black Label to the braai."

DATE: [REDACTED]

THE SECOND YAADT AGE

Historical records from this era become increasingly unreliable. Most documents from this time are just crude drawings of traffic cones and generators.

DATE: CURRENT

THE MODERN YAADT PERIOD

Yaadt appears to have resurfaced. No explanation has been provided. The Ministry of Yaadts, much like Eskom, has declined to comment.
