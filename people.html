DARTH YAADTER

STATUS: Captain
ERA: The Origin
RELIABILITY: 89%

Captain of the first Yaadt Shuttle. Famous for crashing into Planet Yaadt because the ship was in reverse in the dark during loadshedding. Regarded as a tactical genius despite this minor oversight.

GERALD YAADTSON

STATUS: Bureaucrat
ERA: The First Yaadt Age
RELIABILITY: 99%

Assistant Minister of Secondary Yaadt Affairs. Responsible for creating the Yaadtstitution and the Yaadt Census, which famously lost track of the entire population within 4 hours after the power went out.
