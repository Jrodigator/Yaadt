LOCATIONS

YAADT STATION

SIGNIFICANCE: Starting point of the Expedition

A bustling transport hub located beside the intergalactic highway. Features a prominent coffee shop that has never actually opened its doors.

PLANET YAADT

SIGNIFICANCE: Home of the Yaadtrestrials

A planet discovered purely by accident when Darth Yaadter drove his shuttle backwards. The primary export is bewilderment.

THE SCHISM SITE

SIGNIFICANCE: Historical landmark

An completely empty parking lot where the Great Yaadt Schism officially began. It has been preserved as a heritage site, much to the annoyance of local commuters.

THE MINISTRY OF YAADT

SIGNIFICANCE: Seat of Government

A building with an unknown number of floors. According to official documents, the Ministry does not exist, yet it regularly issues passive-aggressive memos.
